Aurora Spring Launch

Today we are introducing a simpler way for teams to organize product work without losing sight of the details.
The spring release brings a clearer project overview, faster search, and a focused review experience designed
for the moments when a team needs to move from an idea to a dependable plan.

The new overview keeps milestones, decisions, and supporting files together. Search now highlights the most
useful matches first, while saved filters make recurring reviews easier to repeat. Review mode removes unrelated
controls and gives comments, approvals, and open questions a consistent home.

These improvements were shaped by small product teams that wanted less ceremony around everyday coordination.
Existing workspaces receive the update automatically, and every new feature can be adopted gradually. Teams can
continue using their current projects while trying the updated views whenever they are ready.

Aurora Spring Launch is available today. Visit the product guide for an illustrated tour, migration notes, and
practical examples covering planning, design review, release preparation, and project handoff.

Build with clarity. Review with confidence. Keep the work moving.
